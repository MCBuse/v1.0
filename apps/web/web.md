## 1. Website Goal

The website should present MCBuse as a credible, early-stage financial infrastructure company building a blockchain-powered micro-banking and low-ticket payment data platform.

The site is not only for one grant or one hackathon. It should serve as the general public-facing company website for:

- Hackathon and blockchain ecosystem visitors
- Merchant pilot users
- Banks, fintechs, PSPs, and payment partners
- Investors
- Grant / accelerator / ecosystem reviewers
- Early users who want to test the demo or join the waitlist

The website should clearly explain:

- What MCBuse is
- Why it was founded
- What problem it solves
- How the demo works
- Who the founders are
- Who should contact or partner with MCBuse
- How merchants and partners can join the pilot

## 2. Core Positioning

### Primary positioning

MCBuse is a financial data infrastructure platform that turns everyday micro-transactions into usable financial visibility.

### Short version

MCBuse captures low-ticket payments, P2P activity, and merchant transaction events, then transforms them into structured financial records that can support visibility, trust, credit readiness, and banking access.

### Recommended homepage headline

**Turning Micro-Payments into Financial Visibility**

### Recommended subheadline

**MCBuse is building a blockchain-powered micro-banking infrastructure that captures low-ticket payments, merchant activity, and peer-to-peer financial behavior — helping people, businesses, and financial institutions connect through verified financial data.**

### Alternative headline for a more merchant-focused version

**From Low-Ticket Payments to Merchant Visibility**

### Alternative subheadline

**A QR/NFC-enabled payment and data infrastructure helping micro-merchants capture transactions, understand payouts, and build structured activity records.**

## 3. Website Tone

The tone should be:

- Professional
- Clear
- Trustworthy
- Fintech-oriented
- Partner-friendly
- Not too “crypto-heavy”
- Simple enough for merchants
- Strong enough for investors and banks

Avoid using language that makes the product sound fully regulated or already market-proven if it is still in demo / sandbox / MVP stage.

Use phrases like:

- “We are building…”
- “Sandbox demo”
- “Pilot-ready direction”
- “Partner-enabled infrastructure”
- “Designed for micro-merchants”
- “Blockchain-powered data layer”
- “Licensed partners handle regulated payment operations where required”

Avoid unsupported claims like:

- “We are a bank”
- “Guaranteed credit scoring”
- “Instant approval”
- “Fully compliant in all markets”
- “Live production payment network” unless already true.

### Section 3 — The Problem

Title:

**Micro-Activity Is Real. The Data Is Missing.**

Problem cards:

1. **Invisible Transactions**Everyday payments and informal activity often create no usable financial record.
2. **Merchant Friction**Micro-merchants face fees, payout delays, reconciliation issues, and onboarding barriers.
3. **Fragmented Financial Identity**Users’ financial behavior is scattered across cash, wallets, banks, and informal systems.
4. **Limited Banking Visibility**Banks and financial partners struggle to assess users and small businesses without structured activity data.
5. **Low-Ticket Inefficiency**Traditional banking and payment systems are not optimized for high-frequency, low-value transactions.

### Section 4 — The Solution

Title:

**A Micro-Banking Infrastructure for Everyday Financial Activity**

Copy:

MCBuse captures micro-financial activity and turns it into structured records that people, merchants, and financial institutions can use.

Core flow:

1. **Capture**QR/NFC payments, P2P activity, merchant transactions, and financial events.
2. **Structure**Convert raw events into clean transaction records with amount, timestamp, status, merchant/user ID, and activity type.
3. **Verify**Use blockchain-backed infrastructure and partner-enabled workflows to support data integrity and traceability.
4. **Activate**Turn activity records into merchant insights, credit signals, partner matching, and financial access opportunities.

Visual direction:

Use a horizontal flow diagram:

User / Merchant Activity

→ MCBuse Capture Layer

→ Data Engine

→ Activity Record

→ Insights / Credit Signals / Partner Matching
