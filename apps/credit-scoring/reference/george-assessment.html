MCBuse Financial Profile Score (0–100) is a transparent, threshold-based score built ONLY from Merchant Activity and Financial Behaviour fields — it does not use Data Reliability or Application-Specific fields, and it is not a statistical PD model. Profile Confidence (Low / Medium / High) reflects how complete the submitted data is and how strong the underlying Data Reliability metrics look — it is a data-quality signal, not a risk signal. The Probability-of-Default / Experimental Credit Score section is clearly marked experimental: it is a logistic regression fit on synthetic data only, four of its factors (business age, capture quality, finalized payments, verified sales) carry a capped policy overlay because the fitted model found no reliable statistical relationship for them, and none of it should be treated as validated until real repayment and default outcomes from financial-institution partners are available. Business age is calculated automatically from the commencement date. Transaction-value figures preserve full cent-level decimal precision (e.g. 475 minor units = €4.75, never rounded to €4 or €5) throughout entry, storage, and the Data records export. Additional Credit Information (collateral, inventory, business/personal debts and assets, external bureau score/report) is captured for lender review only and does not feed any score in this prototype. This is a decision-support estimate, not a lending decision.
